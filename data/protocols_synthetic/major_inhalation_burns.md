## Burn Shock & Inhalation Trauma Escalation
1. IF intra-abdominal hypertension or abdominal compartment syndrome develops during massive fluid resuscitation for burns:
   THEN measure bladder pressure, reduce fluid infusion rate, place nasogastric and rectal tubes, and prepare for decompression.
2. IF severe acute respiratory distress syndrome with severe hypoxemia develops secondary to chemical inhalation trauma:
   THEN initiate lung-protective mechanical ventilation, apply inhaled bronchodilators and heparin-acetylcysteine as formulary-directed.
3. IF severe hypothermia develops during emergency burn care secondary to loss of skin barrier function:
   THEN apply active warming blankets, warm all intravenous fluids, and increase ambient room temperature above thirty degrees.
4. IF severe burn pain causes intense distress, tachycardia, and hypertension in an alert patient:
   THEN administer titrated intravenous opioid fentanyl or morphine as formulary-directed and avoid intramuscular injections.
5. IF high-voltage electrical burn is identified with deep entry and exit wounds:
   THEN monitor continuous cardiac telemetry, hydrate aggressively targeting high urine output, and monitor for myoglobinuria.
6. IF chemical burn exposure is identified with ongoing skin contamination from industrial agent:
   THEN perform copious continuous water irrigation for at least thirty minutes and consult regional poison control center.
7. IF severe burn wound sepsis is suspected forty-eight hours post-injury with focal black discoloration and shock:
   THEN obtain quantitative wound biopsy cultures, debride non-viable tissue, and start broad-spectrum intravenous antimicrobials.
8. IF burn shock fluid resuscitation produces progressive severe facial, conjunctival, and oropharyngeal edema:
   THEN keep endotracheal tube secured with umbilical tape around neck and strictly avoid taping to burned facial skin.
9. IF the major burn patient is stabilized and transfer to a verified regional burn center is arranged:
   THEN communicate total fluid administered, maintain warm transport environment, and provide comprehensive transfer records.
