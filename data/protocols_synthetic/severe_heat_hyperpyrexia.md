## Severe Heat Hyperpyrexia & Multi-Organ Failure Escalation

   THEN administer intravenous N-acetylcysteine as formulary-directed as free radical scavenger and monitor hepatic panel.
2. IF disseminated intravascular coagulation manifests with active bleeding from puncture sites and profound thrombocytopenia:
   THEN administer fresh frozen plasma, cryoprecipitate, and platelet concentrate as formulary-directed under hematology guidance.
3. IF acute oliguric renal failure develops secondary to pigment nephropathy despite initial fluid resuscitation:
   THEN consult nephrology STAT and evaluate for urgent continuous renal replacement therapy.
4. IF hyperkalemia with cardiac conduction abnormalities develops from massive acute rhabdomyolysis:
   THEN administer intravenous calcium gluconate, regular insulin with dextrose, and nebulized salbutamol as formulary-directed.
5. IF arterial hypotension persists despite aggressive active cooling and crystalloid volume repletion:
   THEN initiate continuous norepinephrine infusion as formulary-directed targeting mean arterial pressure restoration.
6. IF acute respiratory distress syndrome develops secondary to systemic inflammatory response syndrome and capillary leak:
   THEN initiate lung-protective mechanical ventilation with low tidal volume and positive end-expiratory pressure.
7. IF hypoglycemia is identified on routine post-cooling point-of-care laboratory evaluation:
   THEN administer concentrated hypertonic dextrose bolus as formulary-directed and monitor capillary glucose hourly.
8. IF the patient develops severe refractory lactic acidosis with arterial blood pH critically depressed:
   THEN optimize tissue perfusion and microvascular oxygen delivery rather than administering empirical sodium bicarbonate.
9. IF persistent coma or non-convulsive status epilepticus is suspected following hyperthermia resolution:
   THEN obtain emergent continuous electroencephalographic monitoring and non-contrast cranial computed tomography scan.
