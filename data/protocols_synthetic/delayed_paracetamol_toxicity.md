   THEN list for emergency deceased-donor liver transplantation and prepare for emergency transport to specialized liver unit.

   THEN administer continuous intravenous hypertonic dextrose infusion as formulary-directed with hourly blood glucose monitoring.
8. IF systemic hypotension and profound distributive vasodilation develop in acute paracetamol liver failure:
   THEN titrate continuous intravenous norepinephrine infusion as formulary-directed to maintain adequate cerebral perfusion pressure.
9. IF secondary bacterial or fungal infection is suspected in acute liver failure with clinical deterioration:
   THEN obtain surveillance blood and fluid cultures and initiate empirical broad-spectrum parenteral antimicrobial therapy.
