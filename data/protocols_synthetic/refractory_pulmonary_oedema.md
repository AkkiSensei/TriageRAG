## Refractory Pulmonary Oedema & Vasodilator Titration
1. IF severe hypertensive pulmonary oedema persists despite initial nitrate boluses AND blood pressure remains high:
   THEN initiate continuous intravenous nitroglycerin or sodium nitroprusside infusion as formulary-directed with invasive arterial monitoring.
2. IF the patient has severe chronic kidney disease and displays diuretic resistance with zero urine output:
   THEN consult nephrology immediately and prepare for emergency continuous veno-venous hemodialysis or ultrafiltration.
3. IF profound hypoxemic respiratory exhaustion leads to hypercapnia and declining level of consciousness:
   THEN discontinue non-invasive mask ventilation immediately and proceed with definitive endotracheal airway intubation.
4. IF concomitant severe right ventricular dilatation and paradoxical septal motion are observed on echocardiogram:
   THEN avoid excessive positive end-expiratory pressure and titrate intravenous fluids judiciously to avoid right heart collapse.
5. IF the patient develops non-sustained ventricular tachycardia associated with myocardial ischemia and congestion:
   THEN administer intravenous amiodarone as formulary-directed and optimize serum potassium and magnesium concentrations.
6. IF acute bronchospasm accompanies pulmonary oedema producing cardiac asthma wheezing:
   THEN administer inhaled ipratropium as formulary-directed and avoid aggressive high-dose beta-agonist bronchodilators.
7. IF systolic blood pressure drops precipitously following vasodilator initiation:
   THEN terminate nitrate infusion immediately, administer intravenous crystalloid bolus, and assess for occult right heart infarction.
8. IF the patient exhibits profound lactic acidosis, oliguria, AND cardiac index severely depressed:
   THEN evaluate for urgent intra-aortic balloon pump or veno-arterial extracorporeal membrane oxygenation placement.
9. IF fever, purulent sputum, and unilateral consolidation indicate superimposed pneumonia triggering heart failure:
   THEN obtain respiratory cultures and initiate empirical broad-spectrum intravenous antimicrobial therapy as formulary-directed.
