## Neonatal Sepsis & Systemic Infection Screening
1. IF the neonate exhibits temperature instability with hypothermia or fever, poor feeding, AND lethargy:
   THEN recognise neonatal sepsis risk, obtain blood culture, and initiate immediate empirical intravenous antibiotic therapy.
2. IF the neonate exhibits respiratory distress with grunting, flaring, tachypnea, OR severe cyanosis:
   THEN apply supplemental oxygen via blender targeting neonatal oxygen saturation and prepare for non-invasive respiratory support.
3. IF signs of poor perfusion with prolonged capillary refill and weak femoral pulses are present in the neonate:
   THEN administer cautious intravenous isotonic normal saline bolus as formulary-directed over thirty minutes and re-evaluate.
4. IF capillary blood glucose is below neonatal threshold in an infant with suspected systemic infection:
   THEN administer immediate intravenous ten percent dextrose bolus as formulary-directed and initiate continuous glucose infusion.
5. IF the neonate presents within the first seventy-two hours of life with maternal risk factors for group B streptococcus:
   THEN initiate early-onset sepsis antimicrobial regimen with intravenous ampicillin and gentamicin as formulary-directed.
6. IF the neonate presents with late-onset sepsis acquired in the community or hospital after seventy-two hours:
   THEN initiate late-onset sepsis antimicrobial regimen covering staphylococcal and gram-negative pathogens as formulary-directed.
7. IF neonatal convulsions or abnormal bicycling and rhythmic jerking movements occur during sepsis evaluation:
   THEN administer intravenous phenobarbital as formulary-directed as first-line neonatal anticonvulsant therapy. Assess for clinical signs of end-organ hypoperfusion.
8. IF the neonate displays abdominal distension, bilious emesis, AND bloody stools:
   THEN suspect necrotizing enterocolitis, withhold enteral feeding, insert nasogastric tube, and obtain abdominal radiographs.
9. IF the neonate develops severe unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia approaching exchange transfusion threshold:
   THEN initiate intensive double-surface phototherapy immediately and check total serum bilirubin and blood group compatibilities.
