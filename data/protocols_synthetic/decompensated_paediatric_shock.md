## Decompensated Paediatric Shock & Inotrope Escalation
1. IF hypotension and shock persist despite fluid boluses and initial catecholamine inotrope infusion:
   THEN escalate epinephrine or norepinephrine infusion as formulary-directed and obtain arterial blood gas and lactate.
2. IF catecholamine-resistant shock occurs in a child with suspected or known adrenal suppression:
   THEN administer stress-dose intravenous hydrocortisone as formulary-directed and draw baseline cortisol.
3. IF the paediatric patient develops severe respiratory failure with worsening acidosis and fatigue:
   THEN proceed with emergency tracheal intubation by most experienced clinician using hemodynamically stable induction agents.
4. IF severe acute metabolic acidosis with base deficit exceeds acceptable limits despite resuscitation:
   THEN optimize oxygenation and circulation, and consider judicious sodium bicarbonate infusion as formulary-directed.
5. IF point-of-care ionized calcium is below age-appropriate reference range during shock resuscitation:
   THEN administer intravenous calcium gluconate or calcium chloride as formulary-directed to improve myocardial contractility.
6. IF refractory cardiogenic shock with poor systolic function is documented on bedside echocardiography:
   THEN initiate intravenous milrinone infusion as formulary-directed with careful monitoring of blood pressure.
7. IF the infant develops supraventricular tachycardia with heart rate exceeding two hundred beats per minute and shock:
   THEN perform emergent synchronized electrical cardioversion as formulary-directed or rapid adenosine if access available.
8. IF tension pneumothorax or pericardial tamponade is identified as obstructive etiology of paediatric shock:
   THEN perform emergent needle decompression or pericardiocentesis as clinically indicated.
9. IF the child remains in refractory septic shock despite triple vasopressor and inotropic support:
   THEN consult paediatric intensive care unit and activate extracorporeal life support retrieval pathway.
