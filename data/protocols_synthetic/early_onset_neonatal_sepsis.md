## Refractory Neonatal Septic Shock & Meningitis Escalation
1. IF neonatal septic shock persists with arterial hypotension despite two volume challenges:
   THEN initiate continuous intravenous dopamine or epinephrine infusion as formulary-directed and place umbilical lines.
2. IF lumbar puncture confirms pleocytosis and suspected bacterial meningitis in a septic neonate:
   THEN escalate antimicrobial therapy to include third-generation cephalosporin or ampicillin with optimal meningeal penetration.
3. IF severe acute respiratory failure with persistent pulmonary hypertension of the neonate develops:
   THEN initiate mechanical ventilation and consider inhaled nitric oxide as formulary-directed in neonatal intensive care.
4. IF the neonate exhibits disseminated intravascular coagulation with bleeding from puncture sites and petechiae:
   THEN administer fresh frozen plasma, platelets, and vitamin K as formulary-directed for active bleeding.
5. IF severe metabolic acidosis persists with blood pH severely depressed despite cardiovascular support:
   THEN optimize mechanical ventilation, ensure adequate tissue perfusion, and consult neonatal intensivist.
6. IF herpes simplex virus encephalitis or disseminated neonatal infection is suspected with maternal vesicular lesions:
   THEN add intravenous acyclovir as formulary-directed immediately pending viral polymerase chain reaction results.
7. IF congenital adrenal hyperplasia is suspected with severe hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, AND ambiguous genitalia or shock:
   THEN administer intravenous hydrocortisone as formulary-directed and correct electrolyte imbalances with monitoring.
8. IF hyperammonemia or inborn error of metabolism is suspected with encephalopathy AND respiratory alkalosis:
   THEN stop all protein intake, administer high-rate intravenous dextrose, and consult medical genetics service.
9. IF oliguria persists despite restoration of mean arterial blood pressure in a septic neonate:
   THEN restrict maintenance fluids cautiously, monitor serial serum electrolytes, and evaluate for acute renal failure.
