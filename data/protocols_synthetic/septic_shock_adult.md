## Adult Sepsis & Septic Shock Resuscitation
1. IF the adult patient exhibits suspected infection, fever or hypothermia, tachypnea, AND tachycardia:
   THEN measure blood lactate, obtain two sets of blood cultures, and evaluate for organ dysfunction.
2. IF hypotension with systolic blood pressure below threshold OR blood lactate elevated is identified:
   THEN initiate rapid intravenous crystalloid bolus as formulary-directed within the first hour of recognition.
3. IF arterial hypotension persists during or after initial fluid bolus resuscitation:
   THEN initiate continuous intravenous norepinephrine infusion as formulary-directed targeting mean arterial pressure.
4. IF blood cultures have been obtained AND severe sepsis or septic shock criteria are fulfilled:
   THEN administer intravenous broad-spectrum antimicrobial therapy as formulary-directed within sixty minutes of triage.
5. IF mean arterial pressure remains below target despite escalating norepinephrine infusion:
   THEN add continuous intravenous vasopressin as formulary-directed as second-line vasopressor to restore vascular tone and mitigate systemic hypoperfusion.
6. IF persistent myocardial dysfunction with low cardiac output and elevated filling pressures is identified:
   THEN add continuous intravenous dobutamine infusion as formulary-directed in consultation with critical care.
7. IF arterial hypotension is refractory to high-dose vasopressor therapy AND fluid resuscitation:
   THEN administer stress-dose intravenous hydrocortisone as formulary-directed to treat relative adrenal insufficiency. Assess for clinical signs of end-organ hypoperfusion.
8. IF severe acute respiratory distress syndrome develops with profound hypoxemia requiring intubation:
   THEN implement lung-protective mechanical ventilation with low tidal volume and permissive hypercapnia.
9. IF an identifiable infected fluid collection, abscess, or necrotizing soft-tissue infection is present:
   THEN obtain emergent surgical consultation for definitive procedural source control within six hours.
