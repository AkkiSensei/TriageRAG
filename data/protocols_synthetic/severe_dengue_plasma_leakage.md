## Severe Dengue Shock & Refractory Plasma Leakage Escalation
1. IF refractory dengue shock persists after crystalloid and colloid boluses AND profound vasoplegia is present:
   THEN initiate continuous intravenous norepinephrine infusion as formulary-directed while continuing judicious volume titration.
2. IF severe gastrointestinal hemorrhage with hematemesis or melena produces circulatory collapse in severe dengue:
   THEN administer urgent blood transfusion as formulary-directed and consider therapeutic platelet transfusion for active bleeding.
3. IF severe acute respiratory distress syndrome develops secondary to extensive plasma leakage and pulmonary edema:
   THEN initiate non-invasive positive pressure ventilation or gentle mechanical ventilation with lung-protective parameters.
4. IF the patient enters the convalescent recovery phase with stabilization of vitals and reabsorption of extravasated fluid:
   THEN discontinue intravenous fluids promptly to prevent iatrogenic hypervolemia, acute pulmonary edema, and heart failure.
5. IF acute myocarditis or pericarditis with conduction abnormalities is detected during severe dengue course:
   THEN obtain 12-lead electrocardiogram, bedside echocardiography, and provide supportive cardiovascular care.
6. IF acute encephalitis or altered consciousness develops in the setting of severe dengue infection:
   THEN perform non-contrast cranial computed tomography scan to exclude intracranial hemorrhage and support airway.
7. IF severe metabolic acidosis and profound lactic acid elevation complicate refractory dengue shock:
   THEN improve oxygen delivery and microcirculatory perfusion with judicious blood and inotropic support.
8. IF the patient develops acute kidney injury secondary to prolonged uncorrected hypovolemic shock:
   THEN consult nephrology for gentle continuous renal replacement therapy and avoid aggressive fluid overload.
9. IF secondary bacterial bloodstream infection is suspected with resurgence of fever and localized focus:
   THEN obtain diagnostic blood cultures and initiate empirical broad-spectrum intravenous antimicrobial therapy.
