# Emergency Protocol: Refractory & Super-Refractory Status Epilepticus (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Refractory & Super-Refractory Status Epilepticus
1. IF refractory status epilepticus continues despite adequate dosing of two antiepileptic drug classes:
   THEN proceed with rapid sequence induction intubation and administer continuous intravenous propofol or midazolam as formulary-directed.
2. IF continuous intravenous anesthetic therapy triggers profound arterial hypotension and vasoplegia:
   THEN initiate invasive arterial line monitoring and titrate continuous intravenous norepinephrine as formulary-directed.
3. IF continuous electroencephalography confirms electrographic burst suppression OR cessation of ictal discharges:
   THEN maintain continuous infusion for twenty-four hours before attempting gradual weaning under electroencephalographic surveillance.
4. IF status epilepticus continues or recurs twenty-four hours or more after starting continuous anesthetic therapy:
   THEN declare super-refractory status epilepticus, consider intravenous ketamine as formulary-directed, and consult neurointensivist.
5. IF the patient exhibits severe hyperthermia, rhabdomyolysis, and dark tea-colored urine secondary to prolonged convulsions:
   THEN initiate aggressive intravenous crystalloid volume expansion and monitor serial serum creatine kinase and potassium.
6. IF isoniazid toxicity is identified or suspected as the underlying etiology of intractable status epilepticus:
   THEN administer intravenous pyridoxine vitamin B6 as formulary-directed in dose equimolar to ingested toxin.
7. IF seizures are accompanied by severe acute hyponatremia with serum sodium critically depressed:
   THEN administer intravenous hypertonic saline bolus as formulary-directed to arrest acute hyponatremic convulsions.
8. IF acute alcohol withdrawal status epilepticus is refractory to high-dose benzodiazepines:
   THEN administer intravenous phenobarbital as formulary-directed under intensive respiratory and cardiac surveillance.
9. IF the patient exhibits persistent non-convulsive status epilepticus with waxing and waning confusion and automatisms:
   THEN obtain emergent continuous electroencephalogram and titrate non-sedating antiepileptic medications as formulary-directed.
