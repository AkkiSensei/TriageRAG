# Emergency Protocol: Severe Symptomatic Hypoglycemia (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Glycemic Resuscitation Steps
1. IF the adult patient presents with acute neuroglycopenic symptoms and confirmed capillary hypoglycemia:
   THEN administer intravenous dextrose immediately via secure vascular access.
2. IF intravenous access cannot be rapidly secured AND patient is conscious:
   THEN administer oral rapid-acting carbohydrates.
3. IF patient remains confused or altered following glucose administration:
   THEN repeat capillary glucose measurement within fifteen minutes and withhold sedatives.
