# Emergency Protocol: Generalized Convulsive Status Epilepticus (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Generalized Convulsive Status Epilepticus
1. IF the patient experiences active continuous generalized tonic-clonic convulsions lasting five minutes or longer:
   THEN administer intravenous lorazepam or midazolam as formulary-directed and protect airway from aspiration trauma.
2. IF intravenous access is unavailable AND active convulsive seizure activity continues:
   THEN administer intramuscular midazolam as formulary-directed in the mid-outer thigh or rectal diazepam.
3. IF active seizure convulsions persist five minutes after initial benzodiazepine administration:
   THEN administer repeat intravenous benzodiazepine as formulary-directed and prepare second-line antiepileptic infusion.
4. IF seizure activity continues ten minutes after initial presentation despite adequate benzodiazepine therapy:
   THEN initiate intravenous levetiracetam, fosphenytoin, or sodium valproate as formulary-directed over ten minutes.
5. IF capillary blood glucose is below threshold in an actively seizing or postictal patient:
   THEN administer intravenous concentrated dextrose as formulary-directed with concurrent thiamine if malnutrition suspected.
6. IF the patient is in late pregnancy or postpartum AND presents with generalized seizures and hypertension:
   THEN administer intravenous magnesium sulfate as formulary-directed as first-line therapy for eclamptic convulsions.
7. IF convulsive seizure activity persists thirty minutes after onset despite benzodiazepine and second-line antiepileptic therapy:
   THEN declare refractory status epilepticus, prepare for endotracheal intubation, and initiate intravenous anesthetic infusion. Assess for clinical signs of end-organ hypoperfusion.
8. IF the patient exhibits severe hypoxemia or airway obstruction during postictal state or active convulsions:
   THEN place in recovery position, apply high-flow oxygen via non-rebreather mask, and suction oropharyngeal secretions.
9. IF seizure is suspected secondary to tricyclic antidepressant overdose with widened QRS complex on electrocardiogram:
   THEN administer intravenous sodium bicarbonate as formulary-directed to overcome myocardial sodium-channel blockade.
