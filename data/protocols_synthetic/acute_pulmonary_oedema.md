## Acute Cardiogenic Pulmonary Oedema
1. IF the patient presents with severe dyspnea, bilateral pulmonary crackles, AND systolic blood pressure is elevated:
   THEN administer sublingual or intravenous nitroglycerin as formulary-directed and initiate non-invasive ventilation.
2. IF the patient exhibits acute respiratory distress with hypoxemia AND respiratory rate is markedly elevated:
   THEN apply continuous positive airway pressure via tight-fitting mask targeting positive end-expiratory pressure.
3. IF systolic blood pressure is elevated above threshold AND signs of fluid overload are evident:
   THEN administer intravenous loop diuretic furosemide as formulary-directed and place an indwelling urinary catheter.
4. IF the patient exhibits signs of acute cardiogenic shock with systolic blood pressure below threshold AND cold clammy skin:
   THEN withhold nitrates and loop diuretics and initiate intravenous inotropic support with dobutamine or milrinone.
5. IF severe tachypnea and accessory muscle use fail to improve after non-invasive ventilation:
   THEN prepare for emergent rapid sequence intubation with lung-protective low tidal volume settings.
6. IF the patient exhibits severe anxiety and air hunger secondary to acute pulmonary congestion:
   THEN provide reassurance and consider low-dose intravenous opioid as formulary-directed under close continuous capnography.
7. IF concomitant tachyarrhythmia with rapid atrial fibrillation is triggering acute hemodynamic decompensation:
   THEN prepare for urgent synchronized electrical cardioversion or administer intravenous digoxin as formulary-directed. Assess for clinical signs of end-organ hypoperfusion.
8. IF refractory hypoxemia persists with arterial oxygen tension inadequate despite maximal non-invasive support:
   THEN escalate positive end-expiratory pressure, consult intensive care unit, and evaluate for mechanical circulatory assistance.
9. IF acute severe mitral regurgitation or papillary muscle rupture is suspected following acute infarction:
   THEN obtain immediate emergent bedside echocardiography and consult cardiothoracic surgery for urgent operative evaluation.
