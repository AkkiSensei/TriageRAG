## Pneumonia with Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome Escalation
1. IF bilateral pulmonary infiltrates with severe hypoxemia develop within one week of severe pneumonia onset:
   THEN diagnose acute respiratory distress syndrome and initiate lung-protective mechanical ventilation with six milliliters per kilogram.
2. IF severe hypoxemic acute respiratory distress syndrome persists with PaO2 to FiO2 ratio below one hundred and fifty:
   THEN place mechanically ventilated patient in prone position for at least sixteen consecutive hours daily.
3. IF refractory ventilator dyssynchrony and high plateau pressures persist despite deep sedation:
   THEN administer continuous intravenous neuromuscular blockade infusion as formulary-directed for up to forty-eight hours.
4. IF empyema is confirmed on pleural fluid analysis with positive gram stain, pH below seven point two, OR purulent fluid:
   THEN place large-bore chest tube for complete pleural drainage and consult thoracic surgery for video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery.
5. IF acute lung abscess with air-fluid cavity develops in necrotizing bacterial or aspiration pneumonia:
   THEN continue prolonged targeted parenteral antibiotic therapy and avoid routine percutaneous drainage due to bronchopleural fistula risk.
6. IF profound septic shock with multi-organ dysfunction complicates severe bacterial pneumonia:
   THEN adhere to international surviving sepsis guidelines with hydrocortisone and vasopressor titration as formulary-directed.
7. IF viral pandemic influenza or coronavirus is confirmed as primary cause of severe pneumonia and respiratory failure:
   THEN administer targeted oral or intravenous antiviral therapy as formulary-directed and enforce airborne infection precautions.
8. IF refractory hypoxemia persists in severe acute respiratory distress syndrome despite proning and maximal ventilatory support:
   THEN evaluate for emergent veno-venous extracorporeal membrane oxygenation retrieval by specialized extracorporeal team.
9. IF clinical improvement with defervescence, radiographic clearance, and extubation is achieved in severe pneumonia:
   THEN complete five to seven days of targeted antimicrobial therapy, de-escalate based on cultures, and arrange outpatient review.
