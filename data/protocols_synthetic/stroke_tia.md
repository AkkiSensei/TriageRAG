## Acute Ischemic Stroke & Transient Ischemic Attack
1. IF the patient presents with sudden acute focal neurological deficit within the thrombolysis eligibility time window:
   THEN perform emergent validated stroke scale evaluation, establish two peripheral lines, and order STAT cranial imaging.
2. IF non-contrast cranial computed tomography scan reveals acute intracranial hemorrhage:
   THEN withhold all antiplatelet, anticoagulant, and thrombolytic agents, and obtain urgent neurosurgical consultation.
3. IF cranial computed tomography scan rules out hemorrhage AND the patient meets all intravenous thrombolysis criteria:
   THEN administer intravenous alteplase or tenecteplase as formulary-directed after verifying absence of contraindications.
4. IF the patient has a large vessel occlusion identified on computed tomography angiography within the thrombectomy window:
   THEN activate emergent endovascular thrombectomy team and transfer immediately to neurointerventional suite.
5. IF systolic blood pressure exceeds thrombolytic ceiling prior to planned thrombolytic administration:
   THEN administer intravenous labetalol or nicardipine as formulary-directed to reduce blood pressure below safety ceiling.
6. IF capillary blood glucose is below threshold in a patient presenting with acute stroke-like symptoms:
   THEN administer intravenous concentrated dextrose as formulary-directed and re-evaluate focal neurological examination.
7. IF the patient develops sudden neurological deterioration, severe headache, or acute vomiting during thrombolysis:
   THEN stop thrombolytic infusion immediately, obtain emergent cranial imaging, and prepare cryoprecipitate as formulary-directed.
8. IF the patient exhibits transient neurological symptoms that fully resolve within one hour of onset:
   THEN calculate validated clinical stroke risk score, initiate antiplatelet therapy, and admit for rapid diagnostic workup.
9. IF hypoxemia with oxygen saturation below target or impaired airway protection is present in acute stroke:
   THEN apply supplemental oxygen via nasal cannula and maintain head of bed elevated at thirty degrees to prevent aspiration.
