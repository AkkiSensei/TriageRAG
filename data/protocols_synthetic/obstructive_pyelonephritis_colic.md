## Obstructive Pyelonephritis & Uroseptic Shock Escalation
1. IF obstructive urosepsis is complicated by severe flank tenderness, rigors, AND elevated lactate (uroseptic shock):
   THEN initiate rapid crystalloid resuscitation, administer broad-spectrum intravenous carbapenem or cephalosporin, and place stent STAT.
2. IF the patient remains persistently febrile and toxic forty-eight hours after ureteral stent placement:
   THEN obtain repeat contrast imaging to rule out perinephric abscess, stent displacement, or fungal colonization.
3. IF renal colic pain is completely refractory to maximal intravenous non-steroidal and opioid analgesia:
   THEN consider regional analgesia, continuous intravenous lidocaine infusion as formulary-directed, or urgent urological intervention.
4. IF large proximal ureteral stone measuring ten millimeters or greater is identified on imaging:
   THEN counsel patient regarding low spontaneous expulsion probability and arrange elective extracorporeal shockwave lithotripsy.
5. IF severe acute kidney injury with creatinine elevated markedly occurs in unilateral stone with normal contralateral kidney:
   THEN investigate for pre-existing occult contralateral renal disease or bilateral nephrolithiasis.
6. IF the patient with suspected renal colic is pregnant and presenting with acute flank pain and hematuria:
   THEN obtain renal and pelvic ultrasound as preferred initial imaging modality and avoid non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs.
7. IF persistent gross hematuria with blood clots causing urinary retention complicates nephrolithiasis:
   THEN place three-way Foley catheter, initiate continuous bladder irrigation, and consult urology for clot evacuation.
8. IF forniceal rupture with retroperitoneal urinoma is documented on computed tomography scan:
   THEN administer intravenous antibiotics, insert ureteral stent for decompression, and monitor for retroperitoneal abscess.
9. IF struvite staghorn calculus is identified in a patient with recurrent Proteus urinary tract infections:
   THEN administer targeted culture-directed antimicrobial therapy and refer for complete percutaneous nephrolithotomy.
