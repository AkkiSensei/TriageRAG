## End-Organ Hypertensive Crisis & Continuous Vasodilator Titration
1. IF blood pressure remains dangerously elevated despite initial intravenous labetalol boluses:
   THEN initiate continuous intravenous nicardipine or clevidipine infusion as formulary-directed with continuous line monitoring.
2. IF severe arterial hypotension or sudden neurological deficit develops following aggressive blood pressure reduction:
   THEN terminate antihypertensive infusions immediately and administer intravenous fluid bolus to restore cerebral perfusion pressure.
3. IF the patient exhibits acute hemorrhagic stroke with severe hypertension AND intracerebral hematoma:
   THEN lower systolic blood pressure smoothly toward target threshold as formulary-directed while avoiding blood pressure variability.
4. IF thrombotic microangiopathy with hemolytic anemia and thrombocytopenia is identified in malignant hypertension:
   THEN control blood pressure aggressively with intravenous agents and consult hematology and nephrology services.
5. IF the patient has severe blood pressure elevation triggered by sympathetic crisis secondary to cocaine or amphetamine:
   THEN administer intravenous benzodiazepine diazepam as formulary-directed to mitigate central sympathetic overdrive.
6. IF the patient develops severe reflex tachycardia during vasodilator infusion with sodium nitroprusside:
   THEN administer concurrent intravenous beta-blocker esmolol as formulary-directed if no contraindications exist.
7. IF cyanide or thiocyanate toxicity is suspected following prolonged high-dose nitroprusside infusion:
   THEN discontinue infusion immediately, obtain arterial blood gas analysis, and administer sodium thiosulfate as formulary-directed.
8. IF severe blood pressure elevation is associated with acute bilateral papilledema and retinal flame hemorrhages:
   THEN admit to intensive care unit for continuous invasive arterial line blood pressure monitoring and gradual titration.
9. IF post-operative hypertension threatens fresh vascular or neurosurgical suture lines:
   THEN titrate short-acting intravenous antihypertensive esmolol or clevidipine as formulary-directed to target strict surgical limits.
