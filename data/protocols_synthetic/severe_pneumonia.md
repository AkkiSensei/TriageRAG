# Emergency Protocol: Severe Community-Acquired Pneumonia (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Severe Community-Acquired Pneumonia
1. IF the adult patient presents with fever, productive cough, pleuritic pain, AND focal crackles on auscultation:
   THEN obtain chest radiograph, blood and sputum cultures, and assess validated clinical pneumonia severity score.
2. IF the patient meets criteria for severe community-acquired pneumonia with respiratory failure OR septic shock:
   THEN admit to intensive care unit and initiate empirical intravenous beta-lactam plus macrolide or respiratory fluoroquinolone.
3. IF hypoxemia with oxygen saturation below target AND marked tachypnea, respiratory distress are present in acute severe pneumonia:
   THEN apply supplemental oxygen via nasal cannula or high-flow nasal cannula targeting oxygen saturation within target range.
4. IF hypotension with systolic blood pressure below threshold is documented in severe pneumonia:
   THEN administer rapid intravenous crystalloid fluid bolus as formulary-directed and start norepinephrine if fluid-unresponsive.
5. IF the patient has risk factors for methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus including prior colonization:
   THEN add intravenous vancomycin or linezolid as formulary-directed to empirical antimicrobial regimen.
6. IF the patient has risk factors for Pseudomonas aeruginosa including severe structural lung disease:
   THEN utilize an antipseudomonal beta-lactam such as piperacillin-tazobactam or cefepime as formulary-directed.
7. IF a significant unilateral pleural effusion is identified on imaging in severe pneumonia:
   THEN perform diagnostic thoracentesis to differentiate simple parapneumonic effusion from complicated empyema requiring tube thoracostomy. Assess for clinical signs of end-organ hypoperfusion.
8. IF severe acute respiratory exhaustion and severe work of breathing persist despite high-flow nasal cannula:
   THEN proceed with emergency endotracheal intubation and lung-protective mechanical ventilation.
9. IF Legionella pneumophila infection is suspected with high fever, relative bradycardia, hyponatremia, AND diarrhea:
   THEN send urinary antigen test and ensure macrolide or fluoroquinolone coverage is actively included in regimen.
