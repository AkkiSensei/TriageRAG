# Emergency Protocol: Major Thermal Burns & Inhalation Injury (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Major Thermal Burns & Inhalation Injury
1. IF the patient presents with thermal burns involving fire in an enclosed space, singed facial hair, AND voice hoarseness:
   THEN suspect acute inhalation injury, perform immediate airway assessment, and secure endotracheal intubation before airway edema closes glottis.
2. IF the patient has partial or full-thickness burns covering twenty percent or greater total body surface area:
   THEN establish two large-bore intravenous lines, initiate fluid resuscitation using the Parkland formula with Ringer's lactate, and assess for systemic hypoperfusion.
3. IF urine output falls below age-appropriate target during initial burn shock fluid resuscitation:
   THEN increase intravenous crystalloid infusion rate by twenty percent hourly as formulary-directed until target urine output restored.
4. IF urine output significantly exceeds target threshold during burn shock resuscitation:
   THEN decrease intravenous crystalloid infusion rate by twenty percent hourly to avoid over-resuscitation and fluid creep.
5. IF circumferential full-thickness burns of the extremities produce loss of distal pulses and cyanosis:
   THEN perform immediate bedside surgical escharotomy along the medial and lateral lines of the limb to restore circulation.
6. IF circumferential full-thickness burns of the chest compromise ventilatory expansion producing severe hypoventilation:
   THEN perform immediate emergency chest wall escharotomy along the anterior axillary lines and subcostal margins.
7. IF carbon monoxide toxicity is suspected with burn in closed space, confusion, and elevated carboxyhemoglobin:
   THEN administer hundred percent high-flow normobaric oxygen via tight non-rebreather mask and evaluate for hyperbaric oxygen.
8. IF cyanide toxicity is suspected in closed-space fire victim with profound unexplained lactic acidosis and coma:
   THEN administer intravenous hydroxocobalamin as formulary-directed immediately as specific cyanide antidote.
9. IF burn wounds are evaluated in the emergency resuscitation bay following initial trauma survey:
   THEN cover burn areas with clean dry sterile sheets, avoid cold water application on extensive burns, and maintain core temperature.
