# Emergency Protocol: Acute Paediatric Dehydration & Gastroenteritis (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Acute Paediatric Dehydration & Gastroenteritis
1. IF the infant or child presents with sunken eyes, loss of skin turgor, AND lethargy or unconsciousness:
   THEN diagnose severe dehydration, establish intravenous or intraosseous access, and start rapid crystalloid resuscitation.
2. IF the child with severe dehydration is in hypovolemic shock with weak thready pulses and delayed refill:
   THEN administer rapid intravenous normal saline or Ringer's lactate bolus as formulary-directed and re-evaluate immediately.
3. IF the child exhibits some dehydration with restlessness, irritability, sunken eyes, AND thirst:
   THEN initiate oral rehydration therapy with low-osmolarity oral rehydration solution as formulary-directed.
4. IF persistent vomiting prevents successful oral rehydration solution intake in some dehydration:
   THEN administer single-dose oral ondansetron as formulary-directed and resume oral rehydration solution fifteen minutes later.
5. IF oral rehydration therapy fails despite antiemetic administration due to severe persistent vomiting or refusal:
   THEN insert nasogastric tube and administer oral rehydration solution at controlled rate or start intravenous maintenance.
6. IF the child has improved after initial rapid rehydration and is now alert and drinking eagerly:
   THEN transition from intravenous fluids to oral rehydration therapy and encourage continuation of age-appropriate feeding.
7. IF severe hypernatremic dehydration with doughy skin turgor and hyperreflexia is documented on electrolyte panel:
   THEN rehydrate slowly over forty-eight hours with monitored fluids as formulary-directed to prevent cerebral edema.
8. IF the child exhibits severe watery diarrhea with suspected Vibrio cholerae infection and dehydration:
   THEN administer oral rehydration or intravenous fluids, and add targeted oral antimicrobial therapy as formulary-directed.
9. IF hypoglycemia accompanies acute dehydration and gastroenteritis in a young child:
   THEN administer intravenous dextrose bolus as formulary-directed and ensure fluids contain maintenance glucose.
