# Emergency Protocol: Acute Chest Pain & Suspected Coronary Syndrome (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Initial Assessment & Hemodynamic Screening
1. IF the adult patient reports acute ischemic chest discomfort:
   THEN obtain immediate 12-lead electrocardiogram and attach continuous cardiac monitor.
2. IF the patient is in severe discomfort AND systolic blood pressure is not low:
   THEN administer sublingual nitroglycerin as formulary-directed.
3. IF the patient has taken sildenafil or tadalafil within the contraindication window:
   THEN withhold nitroglycerin completely to prevent refractory vasodilation.
4. IF ischemic signs persist AND patient exhibits tachycardia without acute heart failure:
   THEN consider administering oral metoprolol if hemodynamically stable.
5. IF hypotension or bradycardia is observed:
   THEN withhold metoprolol and initiate intravenous fluid volume resuscitation.
