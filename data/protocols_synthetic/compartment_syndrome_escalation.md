## Compartment Syndrome & Reperfusion Injury Escalation
1. IF severe ischemia-reperfusion syndrome develops following surgical or catheter-directed arterial revascularization:
   THEN administer aggressive intravenous crystalloid hydration, treat acute hyperkalemia, and maintain high urine output.
2. IF compartment pressure measurement confirms intracompartmental pressure within thirty mmHg of diastolic blood pressure:
   THEN confirm acute compartment syndrome and transport patient immediately to operating theater for four-compartment fasciotomy.
3. IF myoglobinuria is confirmed with dark brown urine AND elevated serum creatine kinase following limb reperfusion:
   THEN administer isotonic crystalloid infusion with urinary alkalinization as formulary-directed to prevent tubulopathy.
4. IF life-threatening hyperkalemia develops with peaked T waves on electrocardiogram following reperfusion of ischemic muscle:
   THEN administer intravenous calcium gluconate, regular insulin with dextrose, and nebulized salbutamol as formulary-directed.
5. IF systemic arterial hypotension and lactic acidemia follow clamp release or thrombolytic limb reperfusion:
   THEN administer rapid intravenous fluid bolus and titrate continuous vasopressor infusion as formulary-directed to sustain perfusion.
6. IF catheter-directed thrombolytic therapy with alteplase is complicated by acute gastrointestinal or retroperitoneal hemorrhage:
   THEN terminate thrombolytic infusion immediately, apply manual vascular compression, and administer cryoprecipitate as formulary-directed.
7. IF severe distal arterial microvascular spasm occurs following catheter embolectomy:
   THEN consider intra-arterial infusion of vasodilators such as papaverine or nitroglycerin as formulary-directed under vascular surgery guidance.
8. IF superficial compartment surgical incisions exhibit signs of extensive tissue breakdown or secondary necrosis:
   THEN perform emergent surgical wound debridement, apply negative pressure wound therapy, and obtain deep microbiological cultures.
9. IF chronic neuropathic pain or ischemic contracture develops in post-ischemic limb during convalescence:
   THEN initiate multimodal neuropathic analgesia with gabapentinoids as formulary-directed and coordinate formal physical therapy.
