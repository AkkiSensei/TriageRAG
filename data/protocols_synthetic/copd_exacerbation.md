# Emergency Protocol: Acute Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease Exacerbation (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Research prototype. Synthetic protocol text only. Not for clinical use. NO numeric doses.

## Acute Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease Exacerbation
1. IF the patient presents with acute worsening dyspnea, increased sputum volume, AND purulence in chronic lung disease:
   THEN administer inhaled short-acting beta-agonists and anticholinergics via nebulizer and apply controlled supplemental oxygen.
2. IF supplemental oxygen is initiated in an acute exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease:
   THEN titrate oxygen via Venturi mask targeting oxygen saturation between eighty-eight and ninety-two percent to prevent hypercapnia.
3. IF acute respiratory acidosis with arterial pH depressed and hypercapnia persists despite initial nebulisation:
   THEN initiate non-invasive positive pressure ventilation with bi-level airway pressure under continuous monitoring.
4. IF the patient exhibits signs of acute infective exacerbation with purulent sputum and systemic fever:
   THEN administer targeted oral or intravenous antimicrobial therapy as formulary-directed covering respiratory pathogens.
5. IF the patient presents with moderate to severe acute chronic obstructive pulmonary disease exacerbation:
   THEN administer oral or intravenous systemic corticosteroid prednisolone or methylprednisolone as formulary-directed for five days.
6. IF the patient fails non-invasive ventilation with worsening respiratory exhaustion, coma, OR hemodynamic instability:
   THEN proceed with emergency endotracheal intubation and mechanical ventilation with prolonged expiratory time to avoid auto-PEEP.
7. IF the patient develops sudden unilateral pleuritic chest pain AND acute worsening dyspnea during exacerbation:
   THEN obtain immediate chest radiograph to rule out spontaneous secondary pneumothorax from ruptured bulla. Assess for clinical signs of end-organ hypoperfusion.
8. IF severe dynamic hyperinflation (auto-PEEP) with profound hypotension develops during mechanical ventilation:
   THEN disconnect endotracheal tube briefly to allow full passive expiration and reduce ventilatory minute volume.
9. IF the patient with severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease exacerbation is recovering and preparing for step-down:
   THEN evaluate inhaler technique, optimize maintenance long-acting bronchodilator therapy, and arrange pulmonary rehabilitation.
